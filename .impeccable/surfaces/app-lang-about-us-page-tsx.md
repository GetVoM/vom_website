# Surface brief: about page (Arabic `/about-us/`, English `/en/about-us/`)

Scope: the about page in both locales, visitor mode Read. Audience: Saudi business owners, accountants and finance staff deciding whether to trust VoM with their books. Job: understand who VoM is, how it was built and who runs it, then register or book a consultation. Proof and content: the old page's content, including its images (owner, 2026-11-02: a first build without them was "a lot of text but very little visual elements"). Each language keeps its own text, typos fixed, minor wording. Constraints: dictionaries hold the copy; `/about-us-2/` is a 301 to `/about-us/` (owner, 2026-11-02); team titles follow the old English page (owner, 2026-11-02); images recoloured to brand hues where they carried lime; sample data in images is labelled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: VoM's story told through its own figures and real pictures. Four real numbers are the page's image and its navigation, a person rises from behind them, and every section below carries a picture or icons. It refuses the category's about page: a mission paragraph and a row of same-size cards.

OWN-WORLD: The homepage's Official Record world: paper white, brand-teal fields with fine guilloche, double-hairline frames, Effra only, small pill buttons, orange only on the primary action. One signature idea, the rolling figures; guilloche stays on teal fields only (owner). Every section is visual: the old page's illustration and photos in rounded frames, and line icons on teal-100 tiles for every list entry. New atoms: poster-scale white numerals on teal, an accountant's double underline ruling off totals.

STORY: The visitor reads the problem in one line, sees four figures roll into place and a business owner rise behind them, and can jump to any figure's chapter. The page then answers the problem, tells the story figure by figure beside the rocket illustration, says what makes VoM different beside a photo, where it is today beside a photo, how it delivers beside the balance card on a teal field, states vision and mission on framed sheets with photos, lists values with icons, shows the three real people, and closes on a sheet with register and consultation actions.
