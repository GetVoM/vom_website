# Surface brief: homepage (Arabic, `/`)

Scope: the Arabic homepage, visitor mode Persuade. Audience: Saudi small-business owners who must become ZATCA-compliant and want accounting without an accountant. Job: understand VoM in one line, see the mechanism, start the free trial on app.getvom.com. Proof on hand: ZATCA approval, 4,000+ establishments, 15 sectors, 4 integrations, plans at 270 and 350 SAR, a content library reviewed by a chartered accountant. Constraints: logo, colours and Effra pinned; Arabic face open; no invented testimonials or numbers; demonstration data labelled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is an official record being issued to the visitor. It refuses the hero-screenshot-plus-feature-cards arrangement: the document is the interface, and compliance is shown by the document completing itself.

OWN-WORLD: Paper white and one unbroken teal field carrying a fine guilloche; deep teal for authority panels; orange only on the primary action and the "today" marker; double hairline frames with corner marks, bilingual micro-labels, serial numbers, a seal, QR blocks, tabular numerals. Effra for everything: Arabic, Latin and digits. Buttons are small, precise, pill-shaped with an arrow.

STORY: The visitor sees their business's compliance certificate being issued, understands that a sale becomes a reported e-invoice and a live report without their hands, and types their own business name into the certificate at the end to start the trial.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Right 7/12: a two-tier headline (light tier, then bold tier), one paragraph, a small orange pill and a ghost link, a one-line proof. Left 5/12: a stack of three documents (invoice, certificate, report) in perspective over the teal guilloche field; on load the invoice slides in, the QR draws, the seal stamps, the "reported to ZATCA" badge lands; then the stack idles with a slow float. Signature interaction: scroll pins the document and the three steps swap its contents; the closing certificate takes the visitor's typed business name.

FORM: Saudi official document grammar, candidate 4 on the ordered grounded list, raised with one drawn flow (zine), one unbroken teal swath and one imperative (WPA poster), hot colour only on the active element (raku), one unbroken "now" line (drum machine). Seed key 0d07585d.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
